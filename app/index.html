grinding
Proof-of-work text search. Each candidate hashes a base with a nonce appended; the grind climbs successively longer prefix matches until the SHA-256 hash spells its target word.
works
Each work is one base and its found words, drawn from the real search records — every hash shown was actually found.
display
16:9 plates that scale to the window. A work runs its grind, pauses on the final state, and repeats.
config
Move the mouse to reveal Config: timing, layout, hash display, colour encoding, and colour scheme. Save SVG exports the display’s exact bytes.
